What stood out to me was how often I had to ask myself what the robot was waiting for. During one manual run, I had already loaded the blue cup, but I kept pressing the load button because I wasn’t sure it had worked. The trace showed me the exact moment the cup reached the tray. I had a similar experience with voice control when the confirmation notification disappeared before I finished reading it. Those moments made me think about robotics from the perspective of the person using it. If someone is relying on a robot to hand them something, they should be able to tell what it heard, what it is holding, and whether it needs permission to continue. If I built a system like this, I would keep the current state and the next action visible long enough for the person to read them at their own pace.
